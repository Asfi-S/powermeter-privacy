Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Eye of Meter respects your privacy. This application does not collect, store, or share your personal information.

Camera Permission

The application uses your device camera only to detect LED pulses on electricity meters for power measurement. Camera images are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server.

Data Collection

The application does not collect:

  • Personal information
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Usage analytics

Local Storage

The application stores only your settings (such as meter constant and measurement duration) locally on your device. These settings never leave your phone.

Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party analytics or advertising services.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer.
